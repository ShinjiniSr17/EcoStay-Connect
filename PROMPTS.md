## Prompt 3 (Best)

### Prompt
I want a peaceful 3-day eco-friendly mountain getaway from Delhi with a budget of ₹7,000. Recommend the best destination, an eco-friendly homestay, a day-wise itinerary, estimated budget, packing checklist, local food to try, and sustainable travel tips.

### Output Summary
The AI generated a complete travel itinerary with destination, accommodation, activities, budget, food suggestions, packing checklist, and sustainability tips.

### Why This Prompt Worked Best
This prompt clearly specified the trip duration, budget, travel style, and expected output. As a result, Gemini generated a detailed, organized, and practical itinerary that required little to no manual refinement.
